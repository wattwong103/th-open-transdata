Notes

TODM focuses on gathering information from free tools and open resources in one place. The intention is to help people find free resources. Some of the sites included might require registration or offer more data for a cost.

I originally created this mapping with a transportation-infrastructure point of view. I would love to include any other resources, especially from fields outside of transportation. Please let me know about anything that might be missing!
